### 🕵️ About Me

😄 &nbsp;Pronouns: **She/her**
🎓 &nbsp;Cybersecurity Engineering student at the Technological University of Panama
🔎 &nbsp;Started out in **Criminal Investigation** — now I apply that same instinct (reconstruct the chain of events, preserve evidence, understand the mechanism before the fix) to digital threats
🧪 &nbsp;I learn by building labs, breaking them myself, and documenting everything — every project in my [portfolio](https://github.com/NicoleS3009/Cybersecurity-Portfolio) started as a question I wanted a hands-on answer to
📍 &nbsp;Based in Panama

### 🗂️ Featured Work

**[Cybersecurity Portfolio](https://github.com/NicoleS3009/Cybersecurity-Portfolio)** — 13+ hands-on labs, from cryptography fundamentals to Active Directory pentesting and Zero Trust architecture. A few highlights:

- 🔴 **AD-CS Pentesting** — chained ESC1→PKINIT to obtain Domain Admin on a self-built lab
- 🔵 **Threat Hunting with Wazuh** — detected and mapped simulated attacks to MITRE ATT&CK
- 🌐 **Zero Trust Wi-Fi Network** — designed and deployed with pfSense + Cloudflare Zero Trust
- 🕵️ **OSINT Attack Surface Analysis** — passive recon exposing real metadata leaks and misconfigurations
